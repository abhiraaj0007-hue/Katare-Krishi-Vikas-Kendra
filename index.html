About Us

Serving Farmers Since 2003

Katare Krishi Vikas Kendra is a local agricultural products and farmer-support store in Tendukheda, Narsinghpur, Madhya Pradesh. Since 2003, we have supplied fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, seed-treatment products, soil-treatment products, cattle-related products and other agricultural essentials to farmers in and around Tendukheda.

We focus on helping farmers access agricultural inputs conveniently, with practical product guidance based on everyday farming requirements.
