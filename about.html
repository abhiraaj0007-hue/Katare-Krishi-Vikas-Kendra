About Us

Serving Farmers Since 2003

A local agricultural store built around one goal: helping farmers in Tendukheda and surrounding areas get the inputs and guidance they need.

Our Story

A Local Agricultural Store for Local Farmers

Katare Krishi Vikas Kendra was established in 2003 in Tendukheda, Narsinghpur, Madhya Pradesh, as a local agricultural products and farmer-support store. Since then, the shop has provided farmers with agricultural inputs including fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, seed-treatment products, soil-treatment products, cattle-related products and other agricultural essentials.

The shop is run by Dinesh Katare, who works closely with farmers in Tendukheda and the surrounding villages to help them access the right agricultural products for their fields and livestock, along with practical guidance based on their day-to-day farming requirements.
